Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how Bolingbroke Patterson ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the GlobalClippz website and the GlobalClippz Uploader, our internal tool for publishing videos to our own YouTube channel.

Who we are

GlobalClippz is operated by Bolingbroke Patterson, United Kingdom. Contact: EA@BolingbrokePatterson.com.

The GlobalClippz Uploader and YouTube API Services

The GlobalClippz Uploader uses YouTube API Services. It is used only by us, to upload videos to the two YouTube channels that we own, GlobalClippz and The Omaha Archive. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

  • What it accesses: with our own permission, it uses Google sign-in (OAuth) to upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to our channels and to add our own videos to our own playlists. It requests only the permissions needed for this.
  • What it stores: a sign-in token for our own account, kept on our own computer, and the YouTube video IDs of videos we have uploaded.
  • What it does not do: it does not read, collect or store personal information about YouTube viewers or any other YouTube users, and it does not share any data with third parties.

By using YouTube API Services through our tool, we are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party connections page.

Visitors to this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and has no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record standard technical information such as IP addresses in server logs for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Emails

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to act on your request (for example a credit change or takedown), and we keep them only as long as needed for that.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us at the address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
